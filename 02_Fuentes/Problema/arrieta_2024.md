## Resumen (de la base de datos, no de la lectura)
The digital transformation explores territories that go beyond the application of digital solutions in the company, it implies: innovation, adaptation of strategies, organizational culture based on values and leadership styles. Organizations must have digital teachers who possess the skills of happy, transformative, inspiring and long-suffering leaders, capable of creating and managing favorable environments for their staff to experience high levels of motivation, commitment and satisfaction. In this sense, paradoxes and leadership characteristics that lead to reformulating the ideal style of management under disruptive levels will be discussed, in which, once its contradictions have been reconciled, we find the absolute essence of the happy leader.
