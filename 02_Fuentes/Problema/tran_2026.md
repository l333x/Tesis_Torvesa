## Resumen (de la base de datos, no de la lectura)
Construction estimators play a critical role in the project bidding process. An accurate estimate thoroughly accounts for the project's scope of work, helping to minimize unexpected costs and risks that could lead to delays. Estimators must overcome numerous challenges, including evaluating market conditions, project schedules, labor expenses, material costs, and contingency factors when preparing cost estimates. Even a minor error can result in significant budget overruns, making it essential for estimators to maintain and review historical estimating data. Previous estimates provide valuable insights that can serve as references for future projects, helping professionals identify opportunities for improvement and reduce the likelihood of unforeseen expenses. This study utilizes data obtained from a journal article to examine the risk factors that influence construction project estimates. The data were collected through a questionnaire and presented in a chart to illustrate both the impact of each risk factor on project costs and its frequency of occurrence.
