## Resumen (de la base de datos, no de la lectura)
The Qatar Construction Industry is not an exception to the business world's long-running use of the term “digital transformation.” The adoption of digital technology is essential for future growth and sustainability in the construction sector, which is a significant contributor to the national economy. The drivers and barriers to digital transformation in the Qatari construction sector are explored in this paper using a review of the literature. Current digitization trends are identified, together with the contextual elements that influence them, including the competitive market, regulatory environment, and client expectations. The research emphasizes the need to overcome cultural, organizational, and technological constraints and a better understanding of the drivers and barriers of digital technology in the construction industry. The paper concludes by proposing that overcoming these obstacles necessitates a thorough and comprehensive strategy for digital transformation that places emphasis on organizational culture, technology integration, and the growth of human capital.
