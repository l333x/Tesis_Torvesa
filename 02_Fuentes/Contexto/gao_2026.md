## Resumen (de la base de datos, no de la lectura)
This study examines how formal knowledge management systems influence the retention and reuse of engineering knowledge in aerospace manufacturing, particularly in aircraft structural design. A qualitative multi-case approach was adopted involving two contrasting organizations: a legacy OEM with a document-centric system and an advanced supplier using a model-based digital thread. The findings reveal a persistent socio-technical gap. While existing systems effectively capture explicit knowledge, they often fail to preserve tacit expertise, design rationale, and informal problem-solving practices. Consequently, engineers frequently rely on personal networks rather than formal repositories when addressing complex design challenges. The study argues that knowledge management systems must evolve into hybrid socio-technical platforms that integrate codified information with distributed organizational expertise. Such an approach can strengthen organizational memory, improve knowledge reuse, and support sustained innovation in aerospace manufacturing.
