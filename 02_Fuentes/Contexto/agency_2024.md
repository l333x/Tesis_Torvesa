## Resumen (de la base de datos, no de la lectura)
This publication provides guidance on developing an effective lessons learned programme to enhance the strategic knowledge management initiatives of nuclear facilities and organizations. Both positive and negative experiences provide opportunities for organizations to learn and improve: the experience gained from individuals and teams, through the conduct of business, events or incidents (both consequential and non-consequential), as well as industry experience. This publication includes case studies from different types of nuclear organizations and supports the development of good practices and successful approaches to lessons learned.
