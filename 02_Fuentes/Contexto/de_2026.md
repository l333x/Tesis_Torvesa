## Resumen (de la base de datos, no de la lectura)
 The Stone Houses (Casas de Pedra) in Nova Veneza – SC, represent a living cultural heritage, transcending mere physical structure to constitute a place of memory and a knowledge center. This article aims to analyze how these buildings and the objects contained within them function as mechanisms for cultural knowledge management and the formation of the Italian community's organizational memory. The methodology employed combined an integrative literature review on heritage, memory, and knowledge management with a descriptive qualitative research approach. Data were collected through semi-structured interviews with two elderly women (G88 and P92) who, upon interacting with the objects in the Stone Houses, evoked sensitive memories and narratives, analyzed from the perspective of sensitive listening. The results reveal that the narratives are strongly marked by the presence of the paternal figure (understood as a symbolic operator), the family nucleus, and work, demonstrating how memory dynamically articulates with knowledge. The study contributes to the understanding of the intersection between cultural heritage, affective memory, and knowledge management, providing insights for the preservation of intangible legacy. Limitations regarding the sample are acknowledged, and future research expansion is suggested, including the exploration of intergenerational transmission and the re-signification of experiences.
