## Resumen (de la base de datos, no de la lectura)
The purpose of this research work is to design strategies that allows OXINOVA SAS to manage knowledge and innovation as strategies that contribute to improve its organizational performance. The description of the problem, the identification of gaps and the current situation of the company were considered to understand how knowledge, innovation and organizational performance are managed. To this end, two instruments were designed to collect information, the first on knowledge management, the second on organizational performance and innovation, which, added to the financial analysis, allowed the construction of the strategies, which was structured through a literature review, case studies tested in similar organizations and field work in the company under study.
