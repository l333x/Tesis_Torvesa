## Resumen (de la base de datos, no de la lectura)
Abstract
               This chapter delves into the substantive legitimacy of technical standards in relation to EU data protection law, specifically the GDPR and its lex specialis the ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC, taking into account its reform. The chapter proposes an analytical framework for technical standards in relation to EU law, categorizing harmonized standards as meta-rules, standards as means for facilitating compliance, and standards for beneficiaries. The analysis looks into issues of controller accountability, the risk-based approach, as well as consent, data security, the right to object and others. Finally, the chapter provides an overview of the legal consequences of referencing standards in data protection related legislation.
