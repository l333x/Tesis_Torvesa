## Resumen (de la base de datos, no de la lectura)
The aim of this research was to analyse the legal treatment and practical application of consumer personal data protection in Ecuador, assessing regulatory progress, limitations in its application and the challenges faced by supervisory bodies and data controllers. A qualitative approach with a documentary-legal design was used, together with inductive-deductive, analytical-synthetic and exegetical methods applied to national legislation, legal doctrine, case law and comparative international regulations. The results showed that, although the Organic Law on Personal Data Protection incorporates modern principles and fundamental rights, its implementation is limited by structural, technical and cultural factors, as well as by low citizen awareness. Institutional weakness in the supervisory authority and deficiencies in business practices, especially in small and medium-sized enterprises, were also identified. It was concluded that there is a gap between regulation and effective compliance, which requires strengthening institutions, promoting digital education and fostering an organisational culture oriented towards privacy protection.
