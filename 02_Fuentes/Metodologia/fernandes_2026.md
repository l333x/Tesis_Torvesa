## Resumen (de la base de datos, no de la lectura)
This article analyzes the application of agile project management in public administration, focusing on the use of Scrum, Kanban and Trello in monitoring agreements and resource transfers. The analysis considers how practices originally disseminated in the private sector can be adapted to the legal and administrative requirements of the public sector. The need for greater transparency, agility and effectiveness in the provision of public services has expanded the search for more efficient ways to organize and monitor the work. The study addresses principles related to the flow of activities, the limitation of ongoing work and visual management. It also presents a case study based on the monitoring of transfer instruments, in which Trello is used as an auxiliary tool to organize the flow from the proposal and formalization of the instrument to the execution, delivery and accountability. Its use is exclusively managerial and does not replace the official procedures, records or systems provided for in the applicable legislation.
