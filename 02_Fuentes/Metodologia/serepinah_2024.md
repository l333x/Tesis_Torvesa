## Resumen (de la base de datos, no de la lectura)
Science education is increasingly integrating elements from different disciplines to increase student understanding and engagement. One approach that is gaining increasing attention is the integration of STEAM (science, technology, engineering, arts, and mathematics) and design thinking. This combination combines scientific methods with creativity and innovation in the science learning process. This research aims to conduct a bibliometric analysis of scientific literature that discusses the integration of STEAM and design thinking in science education. Bibliometric methods will be used to identify key trends, patterns, and research contributions in this area. The results of this analysis provide an in-depth insight into developments and research directions in integrating STEAM and design thinking in science education. It is hoped that the implications of these findings will guide the development of more effective curricula and teaching practices in the context of modern science education.
