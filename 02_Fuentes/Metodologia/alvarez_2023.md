## Resumen (de la base de datos, no de la lectura)
This study addresses the fundamental issues of the audit concept and its application, emphasizing informational auditing and the various models that represent it. On the analysis of the situation of information organizations in Cuba, in terms of the evaluation processes to which they are subjected, the need to prepare a single type of evaluation is based. Based on the study of the structure of information organizations, a comprehensive audit model is proposed that responds to the functional dimension of information organizations in the country at their different levels of performance.
