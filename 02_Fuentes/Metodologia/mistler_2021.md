## Resumen (de la base de datos, no de la lectura)
Organization systems are becoming increasingly complex. Thus, it is becoming more difficult to make the various system elements and their interaction transparent. Different disciplines are needed to solve complex problems in the organization. Therefore, Systems Engineering is needed to find solutions through the involvement of various disciplines. There are many different approaches to Systems Engineering. In this paper, we use the transdisciplinary Generic Systems Engineering approach for the development of organization systems. This implies the use of the enhanced-Demand Compliant Design approach as a transdisciplinary system model. Furthermore, this paper highlights the potential of the information flow analysis to identify the relevant system elements across the entire value chain of an organization system in order to meet requirements. Moreover, the flexible implementation of requirements today demands agility. For this purpose, it is shown how the information flow analysis helps to design an organization system in a modular way to enable flexible adaptations. In addition, it is demonstrated how the modeling of an agile organization system can be achieved with the software iQUAVIS.
