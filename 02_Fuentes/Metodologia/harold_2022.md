## Resumen (de la base de datos, no de la lectura)
In this chapter, the authors explore seven design thinking techniques from prototyping to ethnography to visualization. It shows how design thinking is rooted in three basic mindsets: listening, empathy, and iteration. The chapter discusses the process of recruiting others to an effort. Design thinking is a process, not an outcome. The best design thinking processes tend to have two critical characteristics. They are intentional and iterative. There are different ways to iterate in design thinking. One common way is to alternate between divergence and convergence. Design thinking relies on listening. Anthropologists have listening down to a science called ethnography. Ethnography is just one methodology of qualitative research that is used in design thinking. In a broader process of listening, we affirm the value of the group's time, create ownership, and may uncover more of our own hidden assumptions.
