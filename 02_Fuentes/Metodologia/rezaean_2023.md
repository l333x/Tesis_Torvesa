## Resumen (de la base de datos, no de la lectura)
The objective of this paper is to study a relationship\nmodel between information flow and organizational communication\nsatisfaction dimension. To do so, questionnaires which their validity\nand reliability had been confirmed, were completed by respondents.\nThen the relationship model between mentioned constructs was tested\nby Lisrel 8.8 Software and a structural equation model. Findings show\nthat the suggested model fits well. All paths in the suggested model\nwere significant, so all of the research hypotheses about the positive\neffect of information flow on communication climate (p<0.01,\n=0.98), information flow on organizational perspective (p<0.01,\n=0.82) and information flow on media quality (p<0.01, =1.00) were\nsupported. Therefore, the findings guide the managers to smooth\ninformation flow to utilize organizational communication satisfaction\nconsequences.
